Scope and interpretation

Valid inputs

Every personalized source copy is checked to decode correctly before it enters a mixture.

Native processing

Embedding, averaging, and decoding run at each watermark system's native sample rate.

Speech evaluation

The released study covers aligned speech clips; music and environmental audio remain outside its empirical scope.

Black-box average

Uniform averaging uses no clean audio, gradients, parameters, or decoder queries.

Targeted setting

Target-Bit Margin is a stronger payload-aware experiment and is reported separately.

Preliminary screen

Confidence screening is non-adaptive and should not be interpreted as a complete defense.

Audio files, trial metadata, complete tables, and verification scripts are available in the project repository.
